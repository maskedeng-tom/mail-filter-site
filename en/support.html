Support

How to set up MailMagnet and troubleshoot common issues.

1. General IMAP Setup

If your mail provider isn't listed in the app, enter your IMAP connection details manually.

  • Host: your provider's IMAP server address (e.g. imap.example.com)
  • Port: usually 993 (when using SSL/TLS)
  • SSL/TLS: keep this enabled unless you have a specific reason not to

If your mail provider has two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled, you may need to generate an "app password" instead of using your regular login password. See the provider-specific guides below.

2. Gmail (OAuth2)

Connect a Gmail account using the "Sign in with Google" button in the app.

  1. Enter your Gmail address on the add-account screen
  2. Your browser opens to Google's sign-in screen
  3. After signing in, you'll see a permissions confirmation screen
  4. Make sure to check the scope-confirmation checkbox before selecting "Allow"
  5. Return to the app and confirm the connection succeeded

If the checkbox in step 4 is left unchecked, the correct permissions won't be granted and you'll see an "Authentication failed" error. If this happens, go to account settings, re-authorize, and make sure the checkbox is checked this time.

4. Common Issues

  • Authentication fails: If two-factor authentication is enabled, you need an app password rather than your normal login password. See the provider guides above.
  • Connection times out: Check that the host and port are correct, and that your network firewall isn't blocking IMAP traffic (typically port 993).
  • Gmail shows "Authentication failed": See section 2 above, step 4 — re-authorize and make sure the scope-confirmation checkbox is checked.
