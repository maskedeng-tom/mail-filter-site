Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Information We Collect

Mail Filter does not send usage or analytics data to any external server. The only servers the app communicates with are the IMAP server you configure yourself, and Google's OAuth2 authentication server when you connect a Gmail account. The app does not include any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking of any kind.

Information We Store

  • Mail account credentials (password or OAuth refresh token) are encrypted using your operating system's built-in encryption (Electron's safeStorage, via Keychain on macOS) and stored only on your own device.
  • The data used for classifier training (statistical classifier state, and metadata such as the sender and subject of previously classified mail) is stored only in a local database (SQLite) on your device. The app never stores the full body of your emails. Nothing is transmitted externally or shared with any third party.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share any collected or stored data with third parties.

Gmail OAuth Scope

When you connect a Gmail account, the Google consent screen requests two scopes:

  • https://mail.google.com/: used to fetch, classify, and sort your mail.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email: used to identify the connected Google account's email address, so it can be shown in the app's account list.

Neither scope is used for any other purpose.

When connecting Gmail, make sure to check the scope-confirmation checkbox on Google's consent screen before granting access. If it is left unchecked, the app will not receive the permissions it needs and you will see a connection error.

Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app, or removing a mail account from within the app, deletes the locally stored credentials and training data for that account.
